Collision Detector

Unity 6.3 | C#

This packages provides an independent collision detector component for Unity projects.

Implementation

  • 1. Add the detector of your choices on your GameObject, make sure it has a Collider component.
  • 2. Choose the component you want to detect.
  • 3. In your scripts make a field to reference the collision detector.
  • 4. Use the data from the collision detector
  • 5. UnityEvent are also implemented in the collision detector component.
